## 5. The durable findings

**The durable finding is that a plan can survive sixteen adversarial review rounds and still
contain a test that cannot fail.** It was found by *executing* Task 1, not by reviewing it.
Sixteen rounds of review read for what was wrong; only the run read for what was absent.

**The durable finding is that "resolved" is a claim and "cleared" is evidence** (reeve#47).
Replying to a review thread does not clear it; a thread is cleared by the GraphQL mutation, and
a verdict that reads the reply as the clearance is reading an assertion as a measurement.

**The durable finding is that a merged PR's fix SHAs do not survive the merge.** This repo
squash-merges: 36 `(#N)` subjects and **zero** merge commits at the time of the audit, and
**13 of 18** SHAs cited on `docs/TRACKER.md` are unreachable from HEAD. A tracker that records
per-round SHAs is recording values that expire at merge. Record the squash SHA only.

**The durable finding is that the tracker's `[ ]` is unreliable in one direction.** `[x]` was
15/15 correct; `[ ]` was wrong on **10 of 20**, all of them sitting on merged work — because
the tracker is edited by the PR that *builds* the work and never again after the merge. The
fix is structural, not diligence: STATE must be a projection of `git log`.

**The durable finding is that #44's non-taper was the signal.** 66 findings over 15 rounds with
rounds 10–12 running 5, 3, 5 — no convergence. It was the PR that touched the running
guardian's 2,251-line tick. The founder's taper rule came out of it: ten rounds without
tapering means bring the shape, not the next fix.
